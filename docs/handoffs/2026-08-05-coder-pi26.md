- `extensions/ui-customization/footer.ts` — removed `routeStatus`, `agents`, `readingFor`, `reasonFor` from `FooterState`; removed all stage-row machinery (`stageRow`, `reasonText`, `formatElapsed`, `formatTurns`, `isValidReading`, `hasKnownStage`, `stageIndex`, `isFiniteNumber`, `safeElapsed`, `displayToken`, `statusGlyph`, `REASONS`, `STAGE_ORDER`, `MAX_STAGE_ROWS`, `SENSITIVE_HEADER_PATTERN`, `SECRET_ASSIGNMENT_PATTERN`, `PROGRESS_SOURCES`/`isStale`/`ProgressReading`/`WorkflowSubagentSummary` imports).
